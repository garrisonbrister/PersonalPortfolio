Sissy's Log Cabin Hunting Guidebook Ad

Created for Sissy's Log Cabin's Hunting Guidebook. The purpose of this watch campaign was to advertise to a male audience, specifically hunters, as Sissy's is headquartered in Arkansas. The ad was released in the fall in time for deer and duck hunting season, as reflected in the imagery. Designed directly for client review during my art direction internship at Stone Ward.
